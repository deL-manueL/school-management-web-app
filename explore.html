Undergraduate Courses from UK

With three decades of expertise in training and education, IPMC Training now aims to promote UK Degree. IPMC Training is now an NCC Education Accredited Partner Centre enabling candidates to study in their home countries and obtain admission to final year of IT degree (BSc. (Hons) Business Computing & Information Systems) at NCC partner universities.

About NCC

Established in 1966 as a division of the UK's National Computing Centre, NCC Education started offering IT qualifications in 1976 and from 1997 extended its higher education portfolio to include Business, English Language and Foundation Level qualifications. It is a regulated UK Awarding Body and a global provider of British education in IT and Business.

Post Graduate - Diploma in Business Management

The IPMC College of Technology PG-Diploma in Business Management is an Ofqual regulated qualification that will help you understand and apply up to date management theory and practice in the fields of marketing, information strategy, project management, finance, leadership and entrepreneurship.

Entry Requirements
  • Route 1: Honours degree in relevant subject area
  • Route 2: Honours degree in non-relevant subject + 3 years experience
  • Route 3: Five years work experience in relevant area
Course Units
  • Entrepreneurship and Innovation
  • Strategic Management
  • Corporate Finance and Decision Making
  • International Marketing Strategy
  • Project Management
  • Digital Marketing and Communication
  • Information Systems and Knowledge Management
  • Leadership, People and Change

Level 3 Diploma in Computing (L3DC)

The NCC Education Level 3 Diploma in Computing (L3DC) is an Ofqual regulated qualification. It is an Applied General qualification that allows candidates to demonstrate key transferable study skills, mathematical competency and applied cultural understanding.

Entry Requirements
  • Previously studied in English at secondary school level
  • Valid IELTS score of 5.5 or above or equivalent
  • Suitable for students aged 16-19 and adult learners
Career Opportunities
  • IT Helpdesk Professional
  • Data Entry Clerk
  • IT Support Technician
  • Computer Service Repair Technician

Level 4 Diploma in Computing (with Business Management)

The Level 4 Diploma in Computing (with Business Management) is the first year of the NCC Education degree journey. The qualification is equivalent to the first year of a Business IT degree qualification in the UK university system.

Course Units
  • Skills for Computing
  • Computer Systems
  • Essentials of Management
  • E-Business
  • Computer Networks
  • Databases
  • Designing and Developing a Website
  • Understanding Business Organisations

Level 5 Diploma in Computing (with Business Management)

The NCC Education Level 5 Diploma in Computing (with Business Management) is the second year of the NCC Education degree journey and builds upon the knowledge gained during Level 4.

Course Units
  • Database Design and Development
  • Dynamic Websites
  • Information Systems and Organisations
  • Information Systems Analysis
  • Principles of Business Operations
  • Business IT Project

BSc (Hons) Computing Top-up Degree

The BSc (Hons) Computing Top-up Degree is delivered by NCC Education Accredited Partner Centres, validated and awarded by the University of Greenwich. This programme has been designed to prepare students for employment in the IT sector.

About Greenwich University

The University of Greenwich was founded in 1890, situated on the banks of the River Thames. The university is home to a diverse community of nearly 28,000 students studying in the UK and overseas.
